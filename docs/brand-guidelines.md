# SMK Agama Jerlun Brand Guidelines

## Identity

SMK Agama Jerlun communicates with calm confidence: orderly, warm and purposeful. The digital experience should feel like one school ecosystem for pupils, parents, teachers and administrators.

## Voice

- Use clear Malay, active verbs and short labels.
- Put the task first: `Tempah bilik`, `Buka takwim`, `Cari koleksi`.
- Keep formal information respectful and direct. Avoid promotional language for operational workflows.

## Visual system

- Forest green is the primary institutional color.
- Gold is reserved for active states, key calls to action and important metadata.
- Paper and white surfaces keep information readable.
- Use `Manrope` for headings and `Source Sans 3` for body and interface text. The primary tagline is exactly “Adab Dulu Baru Ilmu”.
- Use emerald #123E35, soft white #F5F8F6, pale green #E5EFE9 and gold #C6A35B. Gold is an accent surface; use dark gold for small text. Use architectural arches, generous space and purposeful reveal motion. Honour reduced motion.

## Asset use

- Prefer genuine school, PSS and activity photography.
- Keep the official school logo inside a contained square, never crop it.
- Pair every image with useful alternative text.
